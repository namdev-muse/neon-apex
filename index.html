NEON APEX

Sprint the skyline. Jump the gaps. Land dead-centre — speed is the reward.

3D graphics couldn't start in this browser — Neon Apex needs WebGL. Try a recent Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a device with graphics support.

How to Play

  • You auto-sprint forward across floating neon platforms. There is no brake — every gap is a decision.
  • Jump with Space / W / Up. Press again in the air for a double jump — the long gaps need it.
  • Slide under the pink overhead bars with S / Down. You cannot jump over them — they hang too low.
  • Switch lanes with A / D or Left / Right to dodge the tall pink walls. Jumping into a wall ends the run.
  • Land dead-centre on a platform to chain a speed multiplier — the chain is the score.
  • Collect gold orbs — the high ones only count mid-jump. Touch a checkpoint ring and a fall sends you back there, not to the start.
  • Fall into the void or hit a barrier and you reset to the last checkpoint. Reach the gold finish gate to clear the track.
  • Your best time is saved per track — beat it. Clearing a track unlocks the next one.

Controls

  • Space / W / Up: jump (again in air: double jump) · S / Down: slide · A / D or Left / Right: switch lanes
  • P: pause · M: mute · R: restart
  • Touch: tap to jump, swipe down to slide, swipe left / right to switch lanes
  • Esc closes a panel · Enter confirms on menus
